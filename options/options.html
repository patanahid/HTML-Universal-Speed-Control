Website Speed Settings

Enter website domains and their custom speeds (one per line).
Format: domain.com:speed
Examples:
example.com:20
game.example.com:50
Full URLs, ports and fractional speeds are accepted and normalised to the hostname. Matching is exact, so include www. when the site uses it.
